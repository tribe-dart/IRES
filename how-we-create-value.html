02 · Energy

Powered by the sun. Free from dumsor.

Every IRES home runs on solar: panels, battery storage and a hybrid inverter that keep the lights, fans and fridge running day and night. Choose solar as your main power source or as backup to the grid. Either way, you can retire the petrol or diesel generator, along with its fumes, noise and fuel bills.

Solar as primary: Run fully off-grid on your own clean power. No monthly electricity bill.
Solar as backup: Stay on the grid and switch to stored solar power whenever the lights go off.
Lower emissions: Clean sunshine replaces burning petrol and diesel.
Peace and quiet: No roaring generator and no exhaust in your compound.
Lower bills: Cool earth walls need less cooling, so the sun goes further.
03 · Water

Your own water.

Borehole water systems: We drill, equip and connect a borehole for your home or community, with storage and solar pumping, so you’re never waiting for the mains.

Site survey and drilling Water storage tank and plumbing Powered by your clean solar system
04 · Sanitation

A cleaner way to handle waste.

Biodegradable septic: Instead of a conventional septic tank, we install biodigester systems that break waste down naturally, with far less emptying and no bad smells.

Natural, bacteria-driven treatment Protects groundwater and soil Low maintenance for years
05 · Design

Designed for the climate, the site and the way you live.

Our designers start with how you want to live, then shape the building around sun, breeze and views. Walls are placed for shade and airflow, and every plan is engineered for rammed earth from day one. Already have an architect? We can build fully in rammed earth, or add rammed-earth façades and feature walls to a concrete frame.

Architecture & 3D visualisation Structural & civil engineering Soil testing & wall sample trials Master planning for estates Mechanical, electrical & plumbing Costing & construction drawings
