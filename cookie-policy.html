Cookie policy · November 2026

Cookies & Local Storage

The IRES website is built to be fast, respectful and honest about your privacy.

No tracking cookies

We do not use advertising trackers, marketing cookies, or cross-site fingerprinting scripts. Our website metrics are powered by privacy-friendly, cookieless analytics that count general visits and page interactions without placing cookies on your device or collecting personal identifiers.

Strictly necessary technical storage

When you submit an enquiry form, Cloudflare Turnstile may store temporary technical security tokens in memory or technical session storage solely to distinguish legitimate human submissions from automated spam bots without puzzle captchas.

The interactive features on our site (such as reveal panels and navigation states) run entirely in browser memory and do not persist to cookies.

Questions

If you have any questions regarding our storage practices, please write to contact@iresglobal.com or WhatsApp us at +233 20 966 9000.
