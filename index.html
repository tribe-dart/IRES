Why rammed earth

Cooler, stronger, kinder to the planet.

Rammed earth is made by compacting damp soil, layer by layer, inside a formwork. The natural earth strata are the finish. We test and stabilise the mix with modern structural engineering to deliver lasting performance across West Africa.

Naturally cool

Thick earth walls absorb the day’s heat and release it slowly, so rooms stay comfortable with less air-conditioning.

Low carbon

Built mostly from soil on or near the site. Nothing is fired, and only a small amount of cement is used to stabilise the mix.

Built for generations

Dense, solid walls that grow more beautiful with age. Some earth buildings have stood for centuries.

Fire & pest resistant

Earth doesn’t burn, and solid walls give termites and pests nothing to eat.

Mould resistant & healthy

Breathable earth walls balance indoor humidity, so damp and mould struggle to take hold. Their mass also blocks outside noise.

Low maintenance

Unlike concrete and block, there’s no endless cycle of patching cracks, re-plastering and repainting. The natural earth strata are the finish.

02 · Energy

Powered by the sun. Free from dumsor.

Every IRES home runs on solar: panels, battery storage and a hybrid inverter that keep the lights, fans and fridge running day and night. Choose solar as your main power source or as backup to the grid. Either way, you can retire the petrol or diesel generator, along with its fumes, noise and fuel bills.

Solar as primary: Run fully off-grid on your own clean power. No monthly electricity bill.
Solar as backup: Stay on the grid and switch to stored solar power whenever the lights go off.
Lower emissions: Clean sunshine replaces burning petrol and diesel.
Peace and quiet: No roaring generator and no exhaust in your compound.
Lower bills: Cool earth walls need less cooling, so the sun goes further.

Heritage

The oldest modern building material.

Rammed earth is made by compacting damp soil, layer by layer, inside a formwork. Each layer leaves a visible stripe, the strata you see in our logo. People have built this way for thousands of years, and some of those buildings are still standing.

4,000+ years ago China Neolithic towns protect themselves with hāngtǔ, walls of rammed earth.
3rd century BCE on The Great Wall Early and western sections are built from rammed earth.
Centuries Africa Earthen architecture shapes the continent, from the Ksar of Aït-Ben-Haddou in Morocco to the Asante traditional buildings of Ghana.
12th to 20th century Fujian Tulou, China Great round communal homes of earth, now a UNESCO World Heritage Site.
13th to 14th century The Alhambra, Spain Granada’s palace-fortress is built largely of tapial, rammed earth.
1970s to today The modern revival Engineers in Australia and North America refine stabilised rammed earth: tested mixes, steel-reinforced where needed, built to code.
2020s A Ghanaian resurgence From Prampram to Tamale, rammed earth is rising again all over Ghana.
