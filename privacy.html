How we protect your data.

1. Who we are

Innovative Rammed Earth Solutions (IRES) is the data controller for enquiries submitted through iresglobal.com. We are based in Accra, Ghana.

2. What we collect and why

When you submit an enquiry form or message us, we collect only what is necessary to evaluate your project and reply:

  • Contact details: Your name and phone number (and email address if provided).
  • Project details: Your country of residence, where you plan to build, your preferred project type (Residential, Commercial & Institutional, Developments, or Not sure yet), and your message note.
  • Proof of consent: The date and time you agreed to this policy, and the consent wording version.
  • Technical context: The page where the form was sent, campaign tags (UTM parameters), browser user agent, referrer, and a cryptographically hashed IP address. We never store raw IP addresses.

We do not collect identity documents, payment details, or personal sensitive data through our website forms.

3. Legal basis

The legal basis for processing your information is your explicit consent under Ghana’s Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843), given when you tick the consent checkbox on our form.

4. Who we share data with

IRES owns its leads and customer records. We do not sell or rent your information. We share data only with trusted infrastructure processors under contract:

  • Amazon Web Services (AWS) — Hosted in Ireland (eu-west-1) for our secure leads database and CRM integrations.
  • Resend — For routing transactional enquiry alerts to our internal sales team.
  • Cloudflare — For content delivery network caching, security headers, and Turnstile bot protection.

5. How long we keep your data

We retain website enquiries for 24 months from the last contact date, unless you become an active client or enter into a construction agreement, in which case records are kept for project warranty and legal compliance. Unsolicited spam records are deleted after 90 days.

6. Your rights

Under Act 843, you have the right to request access to the personal data we hold about you, request corrections to inaccurate details, object to processing, or request deletion of your records.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at:

  • Email: contact@iresglobal.com
  • WhatsApp: +233 20 966 9000
  • Location: Accra, Ghana

We verify that you control the contact phone number or email before sharing or removing records, and respond within 21 days.

7. Cookies

Our website is privacy-first. We use cookieless analytics and do not set tracking or advertising cookies. For more details, see our Cookie policy.
