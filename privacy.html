How we handle an enquiry.

This notice covers the contact form on iresglobal.com. It is the working notice for that form. A lawyer should confirm it before IRES treats it as the final policy. Version 2026-10-26.

When you send the form, IRES stores:

  • Your name, email, phone number, project type, site location, and message.
  • The country you live in, and whether you prefer WhatsApp, a call, or email.
  • The time you agreed to this notice, and this version number.
  • The page address, the site that referred you, and campaign tags when they are present.

We use this to reply to you and to keep the enquiry in the IRES customer records. Those records are stored on Amazon Web Services in Ireland. When email sending is switched on, Cloudflare sends the note to the IRES team and, if you gave an email address, a short confirmation to you.

We keep an enquiry for 24 months after the last contact, unless an active project needs it for longer. You can ask to see, correct, or stop the use of your details by emailing contact@iresglobal.com or writing to 54, 5th Circular Road, Cantonments, Accra. You can also WhatsApp +233 20 966 9000.
